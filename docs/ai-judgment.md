# AI Judgment

RevGuard AI uses AI narrowly and defensively.

## Uses AI

- ambiguous checkout abandonment diagnosis
- recovery-message wording support
- natural-language reasoning stored for audit

## Does Not Use AI

- failure-code mapping
- metrics calculation
- policy decisions
- quiet-hours enforcement
- opt-out checks
- attempt limits
- amount validation
- action enumeration
- webhook validation

## Why

Revenue recovery needs deterministic guardrails. AI helps where ambiguity exists, but safety and financial control must remain auditable, testable, and code-driven.
